The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

Start the game

Choose your own legally obtained Ocarina of Time ROM file, then press Start. The ROM is loaded locally in your browser; this page does not upload it.

Choose your .z64 file to begin.
Save and load

Open the EmulatorJS toolbar/menu and choose Save State to download a save-state file, or Load State to load one you saved earlier. You can also use the game's normal in-game save feature.

Save states are tied to the same game and emulator core. Keep a copy of your state files somewhere safe.
